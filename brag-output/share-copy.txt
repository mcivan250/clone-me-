LINKEDIN / LONGER
────────────────────────────────────────────────
Kampala is full of people making things — music, photographs, films,
clothes, businesses. They're all around each other, and almost never in
the same room.

VILLE is our answer: not an event, a town that moves. Eight stops
between October 2026 and July 2027 — some we build, some we build with
a host who already holds the room, one we simply enter. Same room, same
ticket, everywhere it goes.

Impact isn't a slide at the back. Every stop hires locally, pays a brief
to someone who's never been paid for their work, and leaves two roster
seats in the place it visited. The passport records both halves: what
you discovered, and what the stop left behind.

And brands don't sponsor a night. They take a district — navigation,
meeting, the after — and travel with the town.

First stop: a warehouse in Kampala, 31 October 2026.
