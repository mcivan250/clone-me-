How it's cut

0.3 – 2.0

The ordinary side. Cold grey strip light, THIS SIDE, nothing moving. Held just long enough to be dull — that is the setup.

2.05 – 2.3

The cut. Two hard strobe frames, and the world swaps underneath them. Cold never comes back.

2.3 – 3.9

The room floods. The door blasts open rather than easing, floor pool and shafts come up behind it, Club Guvnor signs in.

5.2 – 7.2

YOUR / ANOTHER / SIDE slam up from behind a mask, one per beat, each one kicking the light bars.

9.3 – 10.3

Tagline clears and RED ROOM rises behind its own mask, on the biggest strobe hit of the piece.

11.4 – 14.1

Hairline draws across, the date bar lands, the monthly line settles under it.

14.4 – 20.0

Held end card on a slow push into the light, with two flickers at 16.2s and 18.4s so it never goes dead while people read it.
