What each photo needs

Aperture
470 × 626 px inside a 1080 × 1350 poster. Supply the shot at 1400 px wide or better so it holds up after the crop.
Crop
3:4 portrait, head and shoulders to mid-thigh. Eyes on the upper third — the light is brightest at the top of the aperture and that is where the face has to sit. Missterious2 are a duo, so frame them two-up with both faces in the upper half rather than stacked.
Background
Plainer is better. The doorway light replaces whatever is behind them, so a busy room fights the effect. A cut-out with a transparent background is ideal.
Grade
Duotone to Red Room red and black — crush the mids, hold the highlight on the face, let the rest drop into shadow. Four shots taken in four different rooms will read as one set once they are graded.
Silhouette cut
A second version of each, filled solid black against the lit aperture. That is the teaser half of the reveal: silhouette first, full photo on the drop.
Permission
Written OK from each DJ to run her image on paid and organic posts, plus the exact @handle and name spelling for the flyer.
