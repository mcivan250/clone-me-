01 — The observation

They're all around us.
They're just never in the same room.

Making music. Taking photographs. Shooting films. Designing clothes. Building businesses. All of it happening at once, across one city, in separate rooms — and almost none of it happening together.

02 — What VILLE is

ville
noun · French
A town.
VILLE: a town with no fixed address. You don't live in it. You turn up.

VILLE is not an event. It's a cultural property that moves.

It travels through places, communities and moments, carrying one identity into each. Some stops we build. Some we build with a partner who already holds the room. Some we simply enter.

The room changes every time. What travels is the rule, the roster, the archive and the passport. That's the property — and it's the thing a brand can enter.

06 — Stops 01–04 · the Kampala block

Four names.
Four energies.

Every stop is a Ville. The word in front says which kind of night it is — so the promise stops being a tagline and starts being the schedule. The names describe the energy, never the address, which is why they travel with the town.

01

Rave Ville

The warehouse · Design Hub, Kampala · Sat 31 Oct

Loud, dark, late. A rave in a room with no social memory, so the first night sets its own standard. Leaves: the first 300 passports and the spec every later stop inherits.

02

Switch Ville

The courtyard · Tupe Hostel, UCU · Sat 14 Nov

A silent disco: three channels, three DJs, everyone switching. No amplified sound into a hostel at night, so the permit stops being the problem. Leaves: paid, credited briefs for student photographers and DJs, at the year's lowest door price.

03

Sunset Ville

The hill · Nkonzi Hill · Sat 28 Nov

Fire, food off the grill, a view. The slow stop — proof VILLE needs a place, not a venue. Leaves: the land exactly as found, and the address of Upstairs Ville revealed from the stage.

Upstairs Ville

The rooftop · address revealed late · Sat 12 Dec

A rooftop with the city underneath. The name is the whole tease: it tells you the night is up somewhere and nothing else. Leaves: the season film, and the only two numbers that matter.

08 — Impact, built in

Discovery.
Contribution.

One half is what a person takes out of a stop. The other is what the town leaves in the place. The passport records both — which is why impact is reportable instead of anecdotal.

A touring property that takes a crowd's money and leaves nothing is a tour. A town that hires locally, pays people who've never been paid for their work and leaves two of its own seats behind is something a place invites back.

Season One prints as designed: paper, ten boxes, discovery only. The contribution column joins in the 2027 edition, once there's a road to record.

09 — The offer

Brands don't sponsor it.
They enter it.

A brand takes a district — one function the town genuinely needs — and travels with it to every stop. It isn't on the night. It's in the town.

Illustrative shapes. Not commitments, and not pitched to anyone.

What you don't get
The rule that makes it worth entering

No branded VIP, no roped section, no better version of the night for anyone. Same room, same ticket — partners included. The moment VILLE has a rope, the reason people came stops being true.

How we report
Proof, not impressions

Who was in the room, what got made, what collaborations came out of it, how many passports moved from one stamp to four, and what each stop left behind — named people, paid briefs, spaces handed back.

11 — Next summer

The match is 90 minutes.
The city is 24 hours.

90 minutes — sold, locked, official 22 hours 30 — nobody owns this yet

In June 2027 East Africa fills with visitors for a month. Every brand in the region will build something around what happens inside the stadium. VILLE is building the rest of the day — and it will have been building it for eight months by the time they arrive.

VILLE uses no tournament names, marks or imagery, and claims no affiliation. The rights are sold. The city isn't.

12 — The numbers

Stop 01 pays for itself
at the door.

Production cost Ticket revenue at capacity

UGX millions, 300 capacity per stop. 01 Warehouse — production 15, tickets 15 (50,000 × 300). 02 Courtyard — 15 / 7.5 (25,000). 03 Hill — 22 / 18 (60,000). 04 Unknown — 30 / 21 (70,000). Season production 82m against ticket revenue of 61.5m; the partner target across the block is 100m.

The gate — 10 October

Stop 01 must be able to run unsponsored. Partner income is upside, not survival — which is what lets GENrise say, in every conversation, that the town happens either way. If no partner signs by 10 October, Stop 01 runs on the reduced budget and the conversation moves to Stop 02.
