The idea

The city is better when we experience it together.

There are so many people here doing interesting things — making music, taking photographs, shooting films, designing clothes, building businesses. They're all around us. They're just never in the same room.

VILLE's job is simple: put people in the same room. Sometimes that room is a warehouse. Sometimes it's a hill, a street, a restaurant, a match, a conversation. The room changes. The job doesn't.

A ville is a town. VILLE is a town with no fixed address. You don't live in it — you turn up.

The layer that records all three. Not a loyalty card and not a ticket — a record of where you've been, what you discovered and who you met. Where you went off the grid. Paper in Season One, with ten boxes to fill. Digital only once there are enough people holding one to justify it.

The 2027 position

Not an official anything. That's the point.

VILLE will not use tournament names, marks, imagery or any language implying affiliation. The rights are sold, the category is locked, and pretending otherwise is a legal problem rather than a creative one.

What isn't sold is the city. Visitors arriving next June won't only need somewhere to watch football. They'll need to know where the young people go, where the music is, where the artists are, what to eat, what not to miss, what Kampala actually feels like. Nobody owns that answer yet.

VILLE is building it a year early, in public, with receipts. By the time the visitors arrive, the archive is deep, the roster is known, the routes are mapped and the passport already works.

Which is also the commercial argument today. A partner coming in now is not buying four parties in Kampala. They are buying first position on the cultural layer of the biggest moment East Africa has had in fifty years — at 2026 prices, before anybody else has noticed it's for sale.

How we report

Not impressions. VILLE reports what a customer could check: who was in the room, what got made, what collaborations came out of it, how many passports moved from one stamp to four. Proof a partner can repeat out loud without being embarrassed.

The rule that protects all of it

Season One is not a rehearsal for 2027 and must not be budgeted like one. Chapter 01 is built to break even on tickets alone, so the property survives a slow start and nothing downstream depends on a partner saying yes by a particular Tuesday.

Nothing in 2027 gets built on a forecast. It gets built on what Season One actually produced.
